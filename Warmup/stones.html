The Odd Stone

Nine stones look the same, but one is heavier.
You can use the scale only twice. Find the heavy stone.

Drag stones onto the left and right pans (or tap a stone to move it).

Press Weigh: the scale tips to the heavier side, or stays level.

After two weighings, choose the heavy stone.

The Odder Stone

Twelve stones. One of them is different, but this time you do not know if it is heavier or lighter.

You can use the scale three times.

Find the odd stone and say whether it is heavier or lighter.

The same rules as before: a sure method earns 3 stars, a lucky answer 2.
